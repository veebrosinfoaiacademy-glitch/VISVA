AI That Teaches, Reminds, and Alerts at the Right Time

VISVA AI Tutor

Every student gets a learning companion after class.

Students can clear doubts, revise concepts, and practice at their own pace, while educators see the topics that need extra classroom attention.

  • Explains concepts in simple language with step-by-step clarity.
  • Generates practice questions for revision, tests, and mock preparation.
  • Shows learning gaps so educators can support the right students sooner.
From doubt to practice to progress.
VISVA AI Calling

Follow-ups happen on time, with people stepping in when it matters.

Your team can automate reminders for upcoming classes, missed mock tests, and pending assignments, then personally connect with students who need care.

  • Calls and reminds students about classes, tests, assignments, and deadlines.
  • Identifies missed activity and repeated delays for quick intervention.
  • Reduces routine chasing so staff can focus on meaningful student support.
Automation for routine work. Humans for real care.
